Straight answers

The questions people actually send.

Can it run my 1.5hp AC?

Often yes, on the working-home size — if we give that AC its own clean circuit and enough battery. We will not promise it from a photo of your outdoor unit. That is what the survey is for.

What if it rains for a week?

The battery covers the hours we sized. After that, the grid or a short generator run fills the gap. Nobody honest sells you a week of full-house AC with no sun and a tiny battery.

Will I still pay NEPA?

If you stay connected, yes — for what you still take from the grid. Solar cuts the fuel and the worst of the outages on the loads we back up. It does not wipe the Disco bill to zero unless we design a true off-grid plant and you agree to that scope.

What happens after I tap WhatsApp?

You get a person, not a form. We ask what must stay on, book a Kaduna visit, count loads and roof, then send a quote with equipment warranties and our 1-year labour cover. No deposit talk before you have seen the numbers.
